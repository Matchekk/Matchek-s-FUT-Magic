# FUT Magic product

FUT Magic is the intelligent operating layer for Ultimate Team grinding. Users
state an outcome; FUT Magic observes local context, builds an explainable plan,
asks for the required approval, compiles to the existing local workflow engine,
and verifies every destructive result.

## Experience layers

1. Contextual EA actions for the task at hand.
2. A compact in-tab run HUD for status, pause, stop, and intervention.
3. A native Chrome Side Panel for Home, Projects, Club, and More.

The old large overlay remains under More → Advanced → Legacy Tools until feature
parity exists.

## Tiers

Internal plan identifiers are `free` and `pro`. Customer labels are FUT Magic
and FUT Magic Pro.

Free includes the strong local solver, protection, Review/Assisted modes, one
active project, project progress, basic club health, explanations, Activity
Guard, and diagnostics required for safe support. Safety is never paywalled.

Free also includes a deterministic Router recommendation for the current
bounded Unassigned state. It answers one question—what should happen next—with
a verified Club/Storage move, keep, pause, or ask-user outcome. The advice is
read-only and separate from the exact Clear duplicates batch plan. Only that
batch plan may move the explicitly shown cards after approval. Neither surface
chooses an SBC target, uses Organizer, submits a squad, opens a pack, quicksells,
or automatically replans. Pro Smart Router may later add multi-step
optimization, but never weaker safety gates.

The first Router slice moves ownership only, so protection, project damage,
scarce-card consumption and replacement-cost objective dimensions are exactly
zero. `reserve` exists in the closed model but is not invented until a future
candidate can actually consume or allocate a card.

Free Card protection is a read-only review of the policy used by local SBC
previews. “Never use” means a hard exclusion. “Try to keep” means a soft
selection signal that may yield to preserve solvability. The review is not a
club-wide optimizer and has no approval or execution command. Missing EA flag
provenance remains visible as unverified and never becomes an eligible/safe
claim.

Free also exposes an explicit FC27 or unknown compatibility state without
treating it as FC26. The current FC27 release boundary is observe-only:
read-only context remains visible while planning, classic-project import, and
workflow actions stay off until local rules and recovery are verified.

Pro is reserved for additional planning value: global project optimization,
multi-step routing, multiple projects, advanced recipes/automation, pack queues,
analytics, Evolution planning, cloud profiles, and compatibility intelligence.

The present public build defines tested Pro contracts only. The truthful state
is “not configured”: there is no account, entitlement issuer, purchase flow or
planner endpoint. Signed-out and offline users retain the complete Free local
product. Future cloud results are proposals and never bypass local protection,
preview, approval, capability or recovery gates.

The Global Project Optimizer is now specified for a future separate private
service, but it is not implemented or available. Its v1 product decision is to
maximize covered squads first, then honor project priority among equal-coverage
solutions, then preserve card value/flexibility lexicographically. The design
does not claim chemistry, FC27 or whole-club optimality beyond the exact v1
request model.

Evolution planning has a tested local graph engine, but remains unavailable in
the consumer product. Synthetic fixtures prove explicit OVR and role-aware
Pareto tradeoffs without declaring a universal best path. Until a lawful live
catalog and verified eligibility/transform provider exist, Home shows exactly:
“Live Evolution data is not available in this build.” The row has no command,
provider setup, demo-player result, sign-in, upgrade, or activation path.

Commercial hardening does not make Pro purchasable. All production auth,
entitlement, account, billing and cloud providers remain not configured; every
Pro-labelled row is disabled and carries no command. The inherited external
player/price adapter is also hard-disabled pending written data rights and a
reviewed consent/validation boundary. The Free local product remains usable
without an FUT Magic account or network service.

## Product honesty

- Do not advertise optimizer coverage, recommendations, or FC27 rules that are
  not backed by deterministic models and evidence.
- Do not call Activity Guard ban protection or present a ban probability.
- Never claim official, EA approved, ban safe, or 100% safe.
- Display: “Unofficial. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Electronic Arts.”

```text
Observe → normalize → context → goal → planner → plan → explain → approve
→ compile → local workflow → EA adapter → verify → checkpoint → update
```
